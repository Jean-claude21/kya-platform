# KYA Business Method

Use the validated method and source material supplied by the owning Direction.

Read [references/method.md](references/method.md) when the request requires the detailed business
method, its required inputs, validation rules or expected output.

## Required behavior

1. Confirm the requested deliverable, audience, owner and approved version of the method.
2. Read only the references needed for that deliverable.
3. Apply mandatory structure, terminology and validation criteria exactly.
4. Identify assumptions and missing factual inputs; never invent company facts or approvals.
5. Produce the requested artifact, then run the method's observable checks.

Do not publish, transmit or approve the result. Those actions remain governed by KYA Platform and
the authorized people. Proposed method changes belong in a new draft version; never alter a
published version in place.
