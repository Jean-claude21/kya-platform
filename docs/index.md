# KYA Platform

KYA Platform is the governed control plane for KYA-Energy Group's digital capabilities. It gives
people and AI environments one controlled way to discover and use approved data, Skills, MCP tools,
applications and standards.

## Guiding principles

- **One identity, contextual authorization:** authentication identifies a principal; authorization
  is evaluated for every action in the active organizational context.
- **Stable foundations, replaceable adapters:** KYA Core, business contracts and audit rules remain
  stable while Frappe and other systems integrate through adapters.
- **AI reasons, software guarantees:** AI explores, drafts and interprets; deterministic software
  validates permissions, schemas, transitions and publication.
- **Sources before interfaces:** reliable acquisition and governed access are prioritized before
  building a dedicated interface for every use case.
- **Artifacts are products:** Skills, MCP servers, connectors and applications have owners, versions,
  integrity evidence and controlled release lifecycles.
